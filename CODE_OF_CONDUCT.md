# Code of Conduct

Last updated: 2026-09-18

Applies from: the release of version 4.0.0.

This is a small hobby repo about free itch.io games, run by one unemployed noob dev, poli0981 (SkullMute), called "the Maintainer" below. Basically: be cool. The Maintainer is an introvert at max level with zero social skills, so let's keep things positive.

The rules below are adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (see [§7](#7-attribution)).

## 1. Our pledge

We as members, contributors and the Maintainer pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation — or how many Steam sales you've survived.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## 2. Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals but for the whole community — and the list (more free games!)

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind. Discussing whether a game should carry the `nsfw` flag is fine; sexual content aimed at people is not.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission (doxxing)
- Spam, or links to malware, phishing or other harmful content
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting — or that would make an introvert close the tab immediately

## 3. Enforcement responsibilities

The Maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

The Maintainer has the right and responsibility to remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, discussions, game suggestions and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

## 4. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: the GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, comments, commit messages and code), anything submitted to the project (for example notes sent with the Suggest form), and the Discord community linked from the [About page](https://freeitchgames.win/about). It also applies when someone is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## 5. Reporting

If you experience or witness abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behavior, report it **privately** to the Maintainer:

- email **legal@freeitchgames.win**, or
- send the Maintainer a direct message through one of the contact channels listed on the [About page](https://freeitchgames.win/about).

Please don't report conduct problems in a public issue. Every report will be reviewed and handled promptly and fairly, and the Maintainer will respect the privacy and security of the person who reports.

Because the project has only one maintainer, if your report is about the Maintainer, you can also use GitHub's own "Report abuse" feature or Discord's reporting tools.

## 6. Enforcement guidelines

The Maintainer follows these community impact guidelines when deciding what to do about a breach of this Code of Conduct:

1. **Correction**
   - *Community impact*: inappropriate language or other behavior that is unprofessional or unwelcome.
   - *Consequence*: a private, written warning that explains what the problem was and why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning**
   - *Community impact*: a violation through a single incident or a series of actions.
   - *Consequence*: a warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with the Maintainer, for a set period of time — both in project spaces and in external channels such as social media. Breaking these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.
3. **Temporary ban**
   - *Community impact*: a serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behavior.
   - *Consequence*: a temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community for a set period of time. No public or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with the Maintainer, is allowed during this period. Breaking these terms may lead to a permanent ban.
4. **Permanent ban**
   - *Community impact*: a pattern of violating community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people.
   - *Consequence*: a permanent ban from any public interaction within the community.

The ladder is a guide, not a promise to go step by step: for spam, malware links or serious harassment, the Maintainer may go straight to a ban (block).
