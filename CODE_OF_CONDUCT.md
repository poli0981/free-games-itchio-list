# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as contributors, maintainers (just me, the unemployed noob dev), and visitors pledge to make participation in this repo a harassment-free experience for everyone — regardless of level of experience, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion, nationality, or how many Steam sales you've survived.

Basically: be cool. I'm introvert max level with zero social skills, so let's keep things positive.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what's best for the list (more free games!)
- Showing empathy towards other community members (if any show up :D)

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, trolling, or insults
- Sexualized language or imagery (keep NSFW in the games, not here)
- Personal or political attacks
- Doxxing or sharing private info
- Spamming sketchy links or malware
- Any other conduct that would make an introvert like me close the tab immediately

## Enforcement
I'm the only maintainer (with Grok as backup), so I'll remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, or PRs that violate this. Repeat offenders get blocked. No warnings required — life's too short.

Instances of abusive behavior can be reported by opening an issue (I'll see it... eventually). All reports will be reviewed and kept confidential.

## Scope
This applies everywhere in the repo: issues, PRs, comments, code.

## Final Vibes
This is just a small hobby repo about free itch.io games. Let's keep it fun, chill, and drama-free. If you're here to add games or fix my mediocre code — you're already a legend.
